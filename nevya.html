📚 Essential Reading for Destination Change-Makers

Succeeding in a modern graduate program in IT Tourism requires a balance of core digital intelligence and strategic destination management capabilities. Below is a curated selection of foundational texts to help new Master's students navigate smart tourism ecosystems, data-driven travel insights, and collaborative hospitality leadership:

🌐 SMART TOURISM & IT STRATEGY

  • "Information and Communication Technologies in Tourism"
    by Juho Pesonen and Julia Neidhardt (Eds.)

    The definitive cross-section of research exploring how AI, big data, and mobile computing are revolutionizing consumer experiences and destination marketing.

  • "Handbook of e-Tourism"
    by Dimitrios Buhalis

    An essential, comprehensive guide to how digital ecosystems, distribution channels, and smart innovations drive competitive advantage in the global travel industry.

📈 TOURISM ANALYTICS & MANAGEMENT

  • "Tourism Information Technology"
    by Pierre J. Benckendorff, Pauline J. Sheldon, and Daniel R. Fesenmaier

    A core academic framework for analyzing how systems like global distribution systems (GDS), analytics, and automation transform operations.

  • "Tourism Analytics: A Primer"
    by Emir Ozeren and Mehmet Ali Koseoglu

    A crucial text for learning how to convert raw visitor metrics, hotel data, and online reviews into actionable strategies for destination stakeholders.
